About Us

Zinhle's Hair Salon is a family-run salon in the heart of Durban. We opened our doors in 2018 with two chairs and a simple promise: every client leaves feeling confident and looking their best.

Our stylists have over ten years of experience in braiding, weaves and natural hair care. We take the time to listen to what you want and advise you on what suits your hair type and lifestyle.

The salon is clean, relaxed and welcoming. Bring a friend, enjoy the music, and let us take care of the rest. We offer free tea and coffee while you wait.

Our Work

From neat box braids and long-lasting knotless styles to sleek weave installations, we create looks for school, work, weddings and special occasions.

Every style begins with a wash and a scalp check, so your hair stays healthy underneath. We use quality products and quality hair, and we never rush a style.

Not sure what to choose? Show us a photo of the look you like, and we'll tell you honestly how long it takes and what it costs.

Most clients book a few days ahead, and weekends fill up fast, so reserve your slot early.
